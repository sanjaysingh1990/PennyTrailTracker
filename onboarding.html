Getting Started

Set up PennyTrailTracker in five minutes

The same walkthrough you'll see the first time you open the app — handy to revisit any time, or to send to someone just getting started.

  1. Try it with sample data first (optional)

    On the welcome screen, tap Preview Demo to explore the app with realistic sample transactions, budgets, and goals already filled in — no setup required, and nothing is saved permanently.

  2. Add your first account

    An account (or "wallet") represents a real bank account, card, or cash balance. Give it a name, starting balance, and currency. You can add more later — one per account, card, or currency you want to track separately.

  3. Set up your categories

    PennyTrailTracker comes with a full set of everyday categories (Groceries, Dining, Transit, and more). Rename, reorder, or add your own from Settings → Categories at any time.

  4. Log a transaction

    Tap the + button. Enter it manually, or scan a receipt / paste text from a bank SMS and let AI fill in the amount, merchant, and category for you.

  5. Create your first budget

    Head to the Budgets tab and set a spending limit for a category or time period — Home will keep you posted on progress automatically.

  6. Personalize Home

    Open the ⋮ menu on Home → Edit Home to show, hide, and reorder sections — net worth, budgets, spending graphs, the Daily AI Summary card, and more — until it matches how you actually want to check in.
