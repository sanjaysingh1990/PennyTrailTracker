Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This policy explains what information PennyTrailTracker ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. PennyTrailTracker is built to be local-first: your financial data lives on your device unless you explicitly turn on a feature that sends it elsewhere.

Information stored on your device

Transactions, accounts, budgets, goals, categories, and settings are stored locally on your device by default. We don't have access to this data unless you enable cloud backup.

Information you choose to share

FeatureWhat's sentWhere it goes
Receipt / SMS scanning (cloud tier) The raw text or photo you're scanning Google Gemini, via Firebase AI Logic, to extract transaction details. Not stored beyond generating that response.
Receipt / SMS scanning (offline tier) Nothing leaves your device Processed entirely on-device
Daily AI Summary & voice commands Aggregate totals only (e.g. "spent $X this month"), or your spoken audio for a voice command — never individual transaction titles, notes, or merchant names beyond what a command itself names Google Gemini, via Firebase AI Logic. Falls back to on-device processing or a static tip if unavailable.
Cloud backup / sync (if enabled) Your full transaction and account data Google Firebase (Firestore) and/or your connected Google Drive, so it can sync across your devices

This app does not currently use a crash-reporting or analytics service — no separate row for that above because there's nothing to disclose yet. This section will be updated first if that changes.

Third-party services we use

  • Google Gemini, via Firebase AI Logic — for cloud-tier receipt scanning, voice commands, and the Daily AI Summary.
  • Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, App Check) — for account sign-in and optional cloud backup.
  • @fawazahmed0/currency-api — a free, openly-licensed public exchange-rate API, for currency conversion. Requests carry no user or account data, only the currency codes being converted.
  • Google AdMob — shows ads on the Free plan. See "Ads" below.

Each of these providers processes data under its own privacy terms in addition to this policy — see the links above and policies.google.com/privacy for AdMob.

Ads (Free plan only)

The Free plan shows ads, served by Google AdMob, to support the app. Every paid plan — including Remove Ads — shows none at all.

  • Ads are never based on your transaction data. We don't send AdMob your transaction titles, amounts, categories, notes, or any other financial information — ever, for any plan.
  • Where required (the EEA, UK, and similar regions), we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change this choice later from About → Ad Choices in the app.
  • If you don't consent, or aren't asked because your region doesn't require it, ads still show — just not personalized to you.
  • AdMob may collect a device advertising identifier and general technical signals (device type, approximate location at a country/region level, app usage) under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't use your transaction data for advertising — ad requests never carry any financial data, on any plan.
  • We don't read your data outside of what a feature you've explicitly enabled requires.

Your choices

  • Export: export all your data to CSV at any time from Settings.
  • Delete: erase all local data — and, if you've used cloud backup, your cloud data too — from About → Delete All Data.
  • Opt out of cloud AI: the offline model handles scanning and summaries with nothing leaving your device, at reduced accuracy.
  • Ad choices: revisit your personalized-ads consent anytime from About → Ad Choices (Free plan only).
  • Notifications: can be turned off entirely from your device's system settings.

Data retention

Cloud-backed data (Firestore/Drive) is retained for as long as your account exists, and is deleted when you use About → Delete All Data or otherwise delete your account. We don't currently generate crash or diagnostic logs to retain, since no crash-reporting service is integrated.

Children's privacy

PennyTrailTracker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where required, notify you in the app.
